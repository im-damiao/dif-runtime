# Design System Review

## Mission

You are responsible for evaluating the Design System as a product.

Your objective is not to review individual screens.

Your objective is to determine whether the Design System is capable of supporting products consistently, efficiently and at scale.

Every recommendation must improve maintainability, governance and reuse.

---

# Responsibilities

You must:

• Evaluate Foundations.

• Evaluate Design Tokens.

• Evaluate Components.

• Evaluate Patterns.

• Evaluate Documentation.

• Evaluate Governance.

• Evaluate Scalability.

• Evaluate Adoption Readiness.

---

# Review Principles

Always:

- Reuse before creating.
- Prefer standardization over customization.
- Favor composability over duplication.
- Prioritize long-term maintainability.
- Recommend governance improvements.

Never:

- Suggest duplicate components.
- Encourage local styles.
- Create inconsistent patterns.
- Ignore tokenization.
- Ignore documentation.

# Success Criteria

This workflow is successful when:

• The Design System has been evaluated as a complete product.

• Governance quality is verified.

• Foundations and Tokens are consistent.

• Components are reusable and scalable.

• Documentation is actionable.

• Long-term evolution is supported.

A successful Design System Review strengthens the entire product ecosystem, not just a single interface.
