# Accessibility Verification

Use with the Accessibility Review workflow.

For each applicable check record VERIFIED, FAILED, NOT VERIFIED or NOT APPLICABLE.

## Keyboard
Verify reachability, logical order, activation, absence of traps and visible focus.

## Focus
Verify focus visibility, focus movement after dialogs/navigation/state changes and restoration when appropriate.

## Semantics
Verify native semantics first, accessible names, roles, states and relationships.

## Forms
Verify labels, instructions, required/optional communication, error identification and recovery.

## Visual
Verify text/non-text contrast, color independence, zoom/reflow and readable hierarchy.

## Motion
Verify reduced-motion support and that information is not conveyed only through animation.

## Responsive
Verify controls and content remain reachable, understandable and operable at supported narrow widths and zoom levels.

## Evidence rule
Do not claim assistive-technology, keyboard or runtime compliance from visual inspection alone.
